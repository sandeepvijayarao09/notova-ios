# Notova Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-07-08**

Notova turns your voice into notes. **Your audio recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries are
created and stored entirely on your device.** They are never uploaded to our servers. We only
process the minimum account information needed to sign you in and, if you choose, sync metadata and
connect third‑party integrations.

## What stays on your device (never collected by us)

- Audio recordings you capture or import.
- Transcripts produced by on‑device speech recognition.
- AI summaries and action items generated by on‑device models.

These are stored locally and are deleted when you delete the note or uninstall the app.

## What we process (only if you create an account)

- **Email address** — to create and authenticate your account.
- **Authentication tokens** — stored securely on your device (Keychain / Android Keystore).
- **Integration tokens** — only for providers you explicitly connect (e.g. Notion), used solely to
  export notes you choose to export.

You can use Notova **without an account** — choose "Continue without an account" — in which case we
process none of the above.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, no ad identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs; no cross‑app/website tracking.
- We do not sell or share your personal data.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — to record audio for on‑device transcription.
- **Speech recognition** (Apple platforms) — on‑device transcription.
- **Notifications** (optional) — local, on‑device notifications only. While a recording is in
  progress, the app may show an ongoing "Recording…" notification (on Android this runs in a
  foreground service so audio capture stays reliable). A recording being processed in the
  background shows a "Processing recording…" notification, followed by a "Note ready" notification
  when your note is finished. These notifications are generated entirely on your device — nothing is
  sent to a server, and Notova uses no push notifications. On Android, the notification permission is
  used only for these local notifications.
