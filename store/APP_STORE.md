## Description — iOS

**Notova turns your voice into notes — and keeps every word on your iPhone.**

Record a meeting, a lecture, an interview, or a quick idea. Notova transcribes
it and writes a clean summary with action items — all using on-device AI. Your
audio, transcripts, and summaries are created and stored on your device and are
never uploaded.

**Capture from any source**
- The built-in microphone
- A paired Bluetooth microphone or headset
- An audio file you already have — import it and Notova does the rest

**On-device transcription and summaries**
- Transcription runs on Apple's on-device speech recognition.
- Summaries and action items are generated by Apple's on-device foundation
  models on supported iPhones. Prefer your own model? You can optionally enable
  a local Gemma model.
- No internet connection is required to record, transcribe, or summarize.

**Private by design**
- Audio, transcripts, and summaries stay on your device.
- No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No third-party tracking.
- The optional Notova account only ever stores your email and syncs note
  *metadata* (title, duration, timestamps) — never your audio or text.

**Use it with zero setup**
- Tap **Continue without an account** and start recording immediately. No
  sign-up, no server, no waiting.
- Create an account later if you want cross-device metadata sync and
  integrations.

**Send notes where you work**
- Export a finished note to Notion today. More export targets (Google, Slack,
  Salesforce) are on the way.

Notova is open source (Apache-2.0).

> Note on summaries: on a device without an on-device foundation model — or
> before you install a local model — Notova still produces a short built-in
> summary so the app is always usable. Install or enable a model for richer
> summaries.

## Description — Mac App Store

**Notova turns your voice into notes — and keeps every word on your Mac.**

The native macOS app is built for the desktop: a single window with Record,
Notes, and Settings. Record from your Mac's microphone or a Bluetooth input, or
drop in an existing audio file. Notova transcribes and summarizes it with
on-device AI, and nothing ever leaves your Mac.

**Highlights**
- Capture from the microphone, a Bluetooth mic, or an imported audio file.
- On-device transcription (Apple Speech) and on-device summaries with action
  items (Apple foundation models; optional local Gemma).
- No account required — Notova opens straight to the Record window. Sign in only
  if you want cross-device metadata sync and integrations.
- Private by design: audio, transcripts, and summaries stay on your Mac. No ads,
  no analytics, no tracking.
- Export finished notes to Notion (more targets coming).

Sandboxed and Hardened-Runtime enabled. Open source (Apache-2.0).

## App Privacy (App Store Connect "App Privacy" section)

Notova performs AI on-device and never uploads audio, transcripts, or summaries.
Configure the questionnaire as follows.

**Does this app collect data?** Yes (only the account email, and only when the
user signs in).

### Data collected

**Contact Info → Email Address**
- **Collected:** Yes
- **Linked to the user's identity:** Yes (it identifies the account)
- **Used for tracking:** No
- **Purposes:** App Functionality (account creation, authentication, and
  cross-device metadata sync)
- **Note for the reviewer / disclosure:** Collected only if the user creates an
  account. Users can run the entire app via "Continue without an account", in
  which case no data is collected.

### Data explicitly NOT collected

Declare these as **not collected** — they are created and stored on-device and
never sent to Notova's servers:

- Audio recordings
- Transcripts
- AI summaries and action items
- Contacts, location, health, financial info, browsing history, search history,
  identifiers, usage data, diagnostics

### Tracking

- **Does the app use data to track users?** No.
- No advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no
  `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription` prompt.

## App Review notes

Paste into "Notes" in the App Store Connect submission.

```
Notova records audio and transcribes + summarizes it entirely on-device using
Apple Speech and Apple's on-device foundation models. No audio, transcript, or
summary is ever uploaded. A small backend (api.notova.app) handles only optional
accounts, OAuth brokering for integrations, and note-metadata sync — it never
receives audio or performs AI.

NO DEMO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED.
- iOS: On the sign-in screen, tap "Continue without an account" to enter as a
  guest. Recording, on-device transcription, and on-device summarization are
  fully available with no server. Account-only features (metadata sync,
  integrations) show a sign-in prompt rather than failing.
- macOS: There is no sign-in gate at all — the app opens straight to the
  Record / Notes / Settings window.

To exercise the signed-in path (optional): create an account in-app with any
email/password against api.notova.app, or we can provide test credentials on
request.

Permissions: Microphone (to record) and Speech Recognition (on-device
transcription) are requested on first use; both have usage strings.

Summaries: If a device has no on-device foundation model available, Notova
falls back to a short built-in summary so the flow always completes. This is
expected behavior, not a bug.

Open source: https://github.com/sandeepvijayarao09/notova-ios (Apache-2.0).
```
